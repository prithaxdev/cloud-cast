What we heard

The brief

A free weather dashboard: current conditions, hourly and 7-day forecasts, a map, and the best hours to head out. It should feel observant, plainspoken and outdoorsy, and move away from the orange-cloud look of another well-known brand.

observantplainspokenoutdoorsy

Distinct from cloud-and-sun weather icons, recognisable at 16 px, works in one colour, keeps the brand orange.

Final design

Sun path

The C is the sun's path across the day; the dot is the sun now.

  • The same idea as the dashboard's sundial and its orange Now mark
  • Keeps the brand orange in one place, with no cloud to confuse with Cloudflare
  • A small cut for 16 to 48 px keeps the sun clear of the C's tip
